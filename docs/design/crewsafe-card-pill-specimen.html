01

The three pill roles

Every pill or chip in CrewSafe is exactly one of three roles. The fill style itself tells you which role you're looking at, before you even read the label.

04

Press-to-fill danger

Destructive actions are outlined at rest and fill with danger on engage (hover here = web; press on mobile). Hover the Reject button.

This borrows --band-high as the danger tone because CrewSafe has no dedicated --danger token yet — pending CVD (colour-vision-deficiency) review.

05

Long-content guardrail

Rule codes and rationale strings wrap inside their row rather than overflow the card or force horizontal scroll — the same guardrail enforced by the merge-blocker gate.

Rule: EXTREMELY_HIGH_WBGT_UNACCLIMATISED_HEAVY_INTENSITY_WORK_SUSPENSION_RULE_V2
Rationale: WorksiteAssessment recorded a WBGT reading in the Extremely High band while an unacclimatised crew member was assigned heavy-intensity work with no scheduled rest break in the preceding sixty minutes, exceeding the exposure ceiling defined for this work-rest category and site elevation profile.
Legend. Colour is always paired with a text label (CVD safety) — no pill or chip relies on colour alone to communicate meaning. Danger tone borrows --band-high pending a real --danger token. Filled = state (needs a decision). Outlined = attribute (classifies). Neutral bordered = entity (names, never coloured).
